    Clinic Catalyst team

    What we do

    • Run the ads and the landing pages, and adjust them every week
    • Edit the film footage and schedule the posts and reels
    • Watch every enquiry, and make sure each one gets a reply and a next step
    • Keep the follow-up messages running until people book in HotDoc
    • Load recall reminders so yearly skin checks never slip, especially before summer
    • Send review requests after appointments, to every patient
    • Check every message and ad against AHPRA rules before it goes out
    • Send you one clear report every Monday
