Your first visit

It starts with a proper look at your skin.

Your first appointment is a consultation. Nothing is booked in until you understand your options.

01
  1. 1

    Book a skin consultation

    Book online, or call (07) 3188 5474 and a real person will help. Dr Ali can see you with or without a referral.

  2. 2

    Talk first

    Tell your doctor what you notice, what you have tried and what you would love to change.

    Bring your skincare list. We will not judge the 11-step routine.
  3. 3

    See your skin up close

    A facial skin analysis photographs your face under different lighting, showing sun damage, pigment and redness that are hard to see in a mirror.

  4. 4

    An honest plan

    Your doctor explains what could help, what to expect, and what it will involve. Sometimes the right answer is "not yet", or "not this".

  5. 5

    Your treatment, and what comes after

    If you go ahead, the same doctors look after your treatment and your aftercare. We check in on how your skin is settling.

Good questions

Asked every week.

Is this doctor-led?

Yes. Your skin consultation is with Dr Colin McTari or Dr Ali, the same doctors who look after skin checks and skin cancer care at SKEEN.

Can I just talk it through first?

Yes. Your first appointment is a consultation. Nothing is booked in until you understand your options and have had time to think.

Will you tell me if something is not right for me?

Yes. Sometimes the honest answer is "not yet", or a different option altogether. Your doctor will explain why.

Do I need a referral?

Dr Ali can see you with or without a GP referral. Call us and we can talk through booking with Dr Colin.

Can I spread the cost?

MediPay is available for treatment. Ask us how it works when you book.

Do you see men too?

Yes. SKEEN offers skin care and treatment for men. You can book their appointment at the same time as yours.
