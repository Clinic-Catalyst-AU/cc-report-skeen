Before we spend a dollar on ads

Where the booking happens decides what we can prove

This is the one technical decision that changes what your reporting can tell you. It costs nothing to decide now, and it is entirely your call.

Option one · bookings stay in HotDoc

Patients keep the booking screen they already know, and nothing changes at your front desk. HotDoc gives us nowhere to place a tracking tag, so the booking finishes where neither we nor Google and Meta can see it.

This rules out conversion campaigns. Google and Meta can only be aimed at clicks and enquiries, because they never learn who actually booked, so they cannot go and find more people like them.

What we can still count every week:

  • Every click that leaves your site for HotDoc, counted as a booking started
  • Every call, through a tracked number on the website and the ads
  • Every enquiry and skin check-in completed on the site

Ads get judged on booking starts and enquiries. If you want reporting on booked appointments, and ads that chase them, the booking has to happen somewhere we can see it.

Option two · the booking is taken on your own site

The patient books on your website, in the system we run for you, and it carries the tracking that HotDoc cannot. HotDoc can still sit alongside it for the patients who prefer it, though bookings made in HotDoc stay untracked.

What that unlocks:

  • Every booking counted against the exact ad and search that produced it
  • Budget moved to the campaigns that book patients, not the ones that look busy
  • People who have already booked stop seeing the ad, so the spend goes to new patients
  • Reminders, recalls and review requests run from the same record

This changes how a booking reaches your front desk, so we map it with you before anything moves.

Either way the campaigns run and the reporting arrives every Monday. Option one reports on interest. Option two reports on booked appointments, and lets the ads learn from them.
