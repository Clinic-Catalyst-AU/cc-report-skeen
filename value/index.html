One thing that has to change

Bookings have to come through your GoHighLevel system

HotDoc is a closed platform. We cannot install a Meta pixel or conversion tracking inside it, so the moment a patient leaves your site to book in HotDoc, the trail stops.

That means nobody can tell you which ad, which search or which page produced that patient. You would be spending on Google and Meta with no way to see what is working, and no way for us to improve it.

It also limits what Google can do. With no booking data coming back, a Google campaign can only ever be a click campaign: you pay for clicks and Google optimises for more clicks, because it is never told which click became a patient. Once bookings feed back, the same budget is optimised toward booked appointments instead. Meta is the same. The pixel never sees the booking, so the ads cannot learn who to go and find more of.

So the booking runs through the GoHighLevel system we build for you, which is where the pixel and the conversion tracking live. Every enquiry and every booking is attributed back to the ad that created it, the campaigns optimise toward patients rather than clicks, and the money follows what actually works.
